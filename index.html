Questions? Answered.

Is this a physical product?

No. It's a 100% digital download. Nothing is posted to you; you get instant access to download the files.

What file formats are included?

High-resolution PNG files with transparent backgrounds at 300 DPI. The Complete Vault also includes SVG cut files, tumbler wraps and editable mockups.

Does it work with Cricut Design Space?

Yes. Upload the PNG and use "Print Then Cut", or use the SVG files (Complete Vault) for vinyl and HTV. It also works with Silhouette, Canva, Procreate and Photoshop.

Can I sell what I make?

Yes. Both plans include a commercial licence to sell physical products you make with the designs (tees, mugs, stickers, décor) and to use them in finished designs. You can't resell or share the digital files themselves.

Can I print them at home?

Of course. Any home inkjet or laser printer works for stickers, party printables and iron-on transfer paper.

Do I need design skills?

Not at all. The designs are finished. Just pick one, resize it and print or press.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep the files forever. No monthly fees.

How big is the download?

The files are split into ZIP folders by style so you can download only what you need. We recommend a computer for the full download.

What if I don't like it?

You're covered by a 7-day money-back guarantee. Just email us within 7 days of purchase for a full refund.
